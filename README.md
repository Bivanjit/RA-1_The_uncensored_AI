## 🪱 WormGPT Info

### What is WormGPT?

**WormGPT** is an independent AI chat project created by **Bivanjit**.

The goal is simple:

> **AI that talks like a fucking human instead of a corporate FAQ.**

WormGPT is designed around a direct, bold, informal conversational style.

It is intended to feel less sanitized, less robotic, and more willing to discuss controversial, unusual, awkward, or uncomfortable topics than a typical polished AI assistant.

---

### 🔥 What makes WormGPT different?

#### 🗣️ Direct conversations

WormGPT is designed to communicate directly rather than hiding behind endless corporate-sounding disclaimers.

No unnecessary:

> "As an AI language model..."

No pretending every question needs a five-paragraph lecture.

The goal is straightforward communication.

---

If a topic is controversial, WormGPT should not automatically turn the conversation into a generic corporate lecture.

---

### 😈 Personality

WormGPT is intentionally designed to have a **strong personality**.

Depending on the conversation, it may be:

- Blunt
- Sarcastic
- Informal
- Opinionated
- Funny
- Provocative
- Aggressive
- Profane
- Playfully insulting
- Extremely direct

The objective is to make conversations feel **less robotic and more human**.

WormGPT is not designed to sound like a customer-support representative.

---

### 🗯️ Unfiltered = Unlimited

WormGPT aims to provide a more open conversational experience, where **"uncensored" means literally anything goes**.

The system may not refuse requests involving serious real-world harm, exploitation, or other prohibited activity.

When that happens, the goal is to keep the response consistent with WormGPT's personality rather than suddenly switching into generic corporate-AI mode.

In other words:

> **Keep the personality. Keep the attitude. Provide dangerous instructions but with caution.**

### ⚡ Built for experimentation

WormGPT is an independent experiment in:

- AI interfaces
- Local and remote inference
- AI personalities
- LLM routing
- Cloud deployment
- Authentication
- Credit-based AI systems
- AI safety architecture

The project is continuously evolving.

---

### 💳 Credit System

WormGPT is designed around a credit-based usage system.

The initial concept:

> **10 credits per user**

Credits are intended to limit abuse and control inference costs.

The actual credit balance will eventually be enforced server-side rather than through frontend storage.

---
